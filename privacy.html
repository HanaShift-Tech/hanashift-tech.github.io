1. Who we are

2. What we collect

When you request beta access

Through the form on our homepage we collect the name, work email address and, optionally, the free-text description of your communication challenge that you choose to send us.

When you use the guided demo

The demo on our homepage runs entirely in your browser. Your choices are not transmitted to us and are discarded when you close the page. We do not create an account or a profile for demo visitors.

When you use the product (beta)

The HanaShift service processes the text you submit on our servers in order to generate a reply, a correction and an explanation. This means the content of the messages you enter is transmitted to and processed by us. We also record basic account data (name, email, organisation) and technical logs needed to operate the service.

Technical data

Our hosting provider records standard server logs, including IP address, browser type and pages requested. These are used for security and to keep the site running.

3. Why we process it, and on what basis

  • To provide the service — on the basis of performance of a contract with you or your organisation.
  • To keep the service secure and working — on the basis of our legitimate interest.
  • To improve our models — only where you have explicitly opted in. We do not use the content of customer correspondence for training by default.
