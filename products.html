Products

One engine, three editions — shaped around who is actually paying and who is actually typing.

EDITIONS

Choose by who you're writing to

Every edition shares the same model and the same explanation layer. What changes is the vocabulary, the scenario library and how the licence is administered.

BUSINESS

HanaShift for Teams

For SMEs, startups and export departments working the Europe–Japan corridor. Bought by the company, used by whoever writes the email.

  • Register selection for client, supplier and internal correspondence
  • Subtext reading on incoming Japanese messages
  • Sector vocabulary packs
  • ESG and SDG reporting language
  • Seat-based licence, central administration
EDUCATION

HanaShift for Education

For universities, language schools and business-Japanese courses. The explanation layer is the product here: students see the grammar behind each correction.

  • Guided exercises by register
  • Explanations tied to the form used, not just a corrected answer
  • Class cohorts and progress overview for teachers
  • Shared exercise sets across a whole course
  • Institutional licence
TRAVEL

HanaShift for Hospitality

For hotels, tour operators and front-of-house staff receiving Japanese visitors, and for European travellers heading the other way.

  • Service-industry phrasing and guest-facing politeness
  • Short exchanges rather than long-form email
  • Fast enough for live front-desk exchanges
  • Sector English for Japanese-speaking staff
  • Per-site licence

BOTH DIRECTIONS

From English to Japanese

You write naturally in English. HanaShift asks who the reader is, produces the message in the right register, and explains what each form signals — so the next email needs less help than the last.

From Japanese to English

For Japanese teams working in English: sector-specific phrasing, and a read on how directness lands with a European counterpart. The same asymmetry runs in both directions.

AVAILABILITY

What exists today

HanaShift is pre-launch and we are selecting a first group of companies to shape the product with us. Pricing is set with that group and published at beta launch — we would rather price against real usage than guess in advance.
